## Corrections and interpretation safeguards

- Theories are evidence-supported explanations; laws describe relationships. Neither is immutable or graduates into the other.
- Molarity uses moles per liter of final solution, not added solvent. Stock X and normality are different conventions; equivalents are reaction-specific.
- Disk zones require organism–drug and method-specific criteria. Raw zones cannot rank different antibiotics. Training thresholds are explicitly fictional.
- ATP yield is variable; fermentation regenerates NAD+ and differs from anaerobic respiration. Calvin-cycle reactions fix carbon and use light-reaction products.
- Selectable markers do not verify an insert or protein function. Array dye colors depend on sample assignment; undetected signal does not prove absolute absence.
- Immunodiffusion identity is relative to antibody specificity; partial-identity spurs point toward the antigen lacking the additional determinant. Failed positive controls preclude negative interpretation.
- Laboratory instructions remain governed by approved local SOPs and containment; educational blood typing does not authorize transfusion decisions.
